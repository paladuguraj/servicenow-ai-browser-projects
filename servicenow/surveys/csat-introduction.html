Questions 1–4 use a 1–5 Likert scale for easy quantification and KPI tracking.

Question 5 is an open-ended field to capture qualitative feedback that scores alone can't reveal.

The survey intentionally maps to key NOC performance pillars:

🔵  Q1 → Overall Experience
🟡  Q2 → SLO / Timeliness Performance
🟠  Q3 → Communication Quality
🟢  Q4 → RCA & Preventative Confidence
⚪  Q5 → Voice of the Customer
